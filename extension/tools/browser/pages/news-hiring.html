Tech hiring picked up in the third quarter

Job postings across the technology sector rose eleven per cent between July and September, the first sustained rise in two years, according to figures published on Tuesday. The increase was concentrated in infrastructure and security roles, while front-end and design postings continued to fall.

Recruiters said the shift reflects where budgets have moved rather than a broad recovery. "Companies are hiring people who keep systems running," one said. "They are not hiring people to build new products yet." Several large employers have kept headcount flat while replacing departures.

Requirements in the postings have also changed. Employers increasingly ask for years of experience in production operations, and mention on-call responsibilities earlier in the description. Salary bands published in postings rose four per cent year on year, below inflation over the same period.

The figures cover postings on the five largest job boards and exclude internal listings. Analysts cautioned that a single quarter is not a trend, and that the same figures fell sharply in the equivalent quarter last year before recovering.

Senior Platform Engineer

About the role

Halberd runs the settlement layer for a dozen payment providers. We are hiring a senior platform engineer to own the reliability of that layer. This is a full-time role, and the salary band is published below.

What you'll do

  • Own and operate the settlement service end to end.
  • Take part in the on call rotation for a critical service.
  • Lead the move to smaller services already under way.
  • Help other engineers grow through review and mentoring.

Requirements

  • Five years of experience or more of production backend work.
  • Strong Go, used at work and not only in side projects.
  • Kubernetes in production, including writing operators.
  • Built and ran a streaming pipeline handling real traffic.
  • Postgres tuning and replication.
  • Terraform or similar infrastructure as code.

Nice to have

  • Experience with payment systems or financial reconciliation.
  • Public speaking or written technical communication.

Qualifications

We care about what you have built and run, not where you studied. Show us the systems.

Benefits

Private health cover, a learning budget, and thirty days of leave on top of public holidays. We are remote first across the UK and Ireland.
